My stories
----------

Story 4 - Control player turns
  players.js: the three players with a round total and a bank each.
    currentPlayer() returns whose turn it is, passTurn() moves to the next
    player and wraps back to Player 1, and renderPlayers() redraws the
    player list with the current player highlighted.
  game.js: endTurn() calls passTurn() after a miss, a wrong solve or a
    Lose a Turn. The Next Puzzle button also passes the turn.

Story 5 - Spin wheel and show result
  game.js: handleSpinResult() listens for the spinresult event from
    main.js. On a cash wedge it saves the wedge value and asks the player
    to guess a consonant.

Story 9 - Show previously guessed letters
  letters.js: hasBeenGuessed() and markGuessed() track every letter called
    this round. renderUsedLetters() shows the alphabet and greys out used
    letters, and clearGuessedLetters() resets it when a new puzzle starts.

Story 10 - Enforce Bankrupt and Lose a Turn
  players.js: goBankrupt() sets the current player's round total to $0.
    Their bank is not touched.
  game.js: handleSpinResult() calls goBankrupt() and ends the turn on
    Bankrupt, and just ends the turn on Lose a Turn.

Story 12 - Bank winnings after a solved puzzle
  players.js: bankWinnings() moves the solver's round total into their
    bank and sets every round total back to $0.
  game.js: after a correct solve, the Next Puzzle button passes the turn
    and starts the next round.
