Fortune Tellers: Wheel of Fortune - Sprint 1
Trajan Womack

My stories
----------

Story 2 - Choose a clue and display the puzzle board
  puzzles.js: PUZZLES, the list of category and puzzle pairs.
  board.js: showPuzzle() builds the board, splitIntoRows() wraps whole words
    onto rows of up to 14 tiles, createTile() makes hidden letter tiles and
    shows spaces and punctuation right away.
  game.js: startPuzzle() picks a random puzzle and shows it. It also adds 1
    to the round counter and calls setRound() from main.js so the wheel
    shows that round's top value (the round values are part of story 3).
  index.html: #category and #board elements, .tile styles.

Story 7 - Guess a consonant and update results
  game.js: guessConsonant() checks the letter, reveals it and adds
    letter count x wedge value to the player's round total. A miss calls
    endTurn() so the next player goes.
  board.js: revealLetter() flips matching tiles and returns the count.
  players.js: addToRoundTotal().

Story 8 - Buy a vowel and update results
  game.js: buyVowel() and VOWEL_COST ($500). The player has to have at least
    $500 in their round total. A vowel that isn't in the puzzle still costs
    $500 and passes the turn.
  board.js: hasHiddenVowels() checks if any vowel tile is still hidden.
    Once the last vowel is revealed, the game says "There are no more vowels
    in this puzzle." and the vowel box is turned off with "No vowels left".
    A vowel guess after that is rejected and no money is taken.

Story 11 - Check the puzzle solution
  board.js: isCorrectSolution() compares letters only, so case, spaces and
    punctuation don't matter. revealAll() shows the whole board.
  game.js: solvePuzzle() reveals the board on a correct solve and passes
    the turn on a wrong one.

How to run
----------
The game uses JavaScript modules, so it has to be served over HTTP.
From this folder run:

  python -m http.server 8000

then open http://localhost:8000 in a browser. Three.js loads from a CDN,
so you need an internet connection.
